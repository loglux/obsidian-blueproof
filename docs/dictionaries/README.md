# Example dictionaries

Notes to copy into your dictionary folder (`Dictionaries` by default): words of mathematics and statistics that the
British English spelling dictionary does not know.

The mathematics dictionary includes words from [Acamedic](https://github.com/emareg/acamedic) by emareg, licensed
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); they were chosen and changed as the note's first comment says.
